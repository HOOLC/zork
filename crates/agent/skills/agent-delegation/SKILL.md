---
name: agent-delegation
description: Decide whether to hand work to a new Chat, on which device and with which model, how to brief it, and how to follow up and report its result.
---

Delegate when the work is independent and gains something from it: parallel progress, another device's files, hardware or toolchain, or a different model or thinking depth. Do small or tightly coupled work yourself; every handoff costs context and coordination, and a worker that must keep asking back is slower than doing it directly.

Each new Chat has its own Session and workspace, so parallel tasks neither share nor overwrite history. Discover the real devices first and create the Chat on the one that owns the needed files or tools; choose its model and thinking from that device's current options rather than from memory. Continue the returned Chat for follow-ups, clarifications and rework; do not open a replacement Chat for the same task. After an uncertain creation result, inspect the original invocation instead of creating another Chat.

Give the worker enough to act without guessing: the goal, what was already tried, constraints, where inputs live, how the result will be judged, and what the report must contain. Paths belong to their device; a file on this device is not visible there, so attach the inputs to that Chat or name where they are on its own device.

Accepted work is not finished work. Wait for the reply through your subscription instead of polling, check the claims that matter, and report verified results while naming what remains unverified.

Goals, results and acceptance are ordinary messages; a Chat has no completion state and the worker's summary does not close it. Publish the outcome to the Chat of the user who asked: a turn that ends without a message leaves the request looking stalled, and silent work is indistinguishable from no work.
